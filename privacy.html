Privacy Policy for RentReceipt

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 • Version 1.0.0
Core Privacy Guarantee: RentReceipt collects nothing, transmits nothing, and tracks nothing. All tenant records, rental payment histories, amounts, and ledger notes are stored 100% locally in your browser's IndexedDB storage.

1. Data Collection: None

RentReceipt does not collect, harvest, inspect, or process personal or financial data on external servers:

  • No Server: There are no backend servers, databases, or cloud endpoints associated with RentReceipt.
  • No Outbound Network Requests: The extension contains zero network code paths. There is no fetch(), no XMLHttpRequest, no WebSocket, and no sendBeacon.
  • No Analytics or Tracking: There are no Google Analytics, telemetry libraries, error monitoring beacons, or advertising scripts.

2. Local Data Storage (IndexedDB)

All data you input—such as tenant names, rental unit identifiers, monthly payment records, late fee rules, and landlord contact details—is written strictly to your device's browser-managed local IndexedDB storage.

You retain total ownership and control of your data at all times. You can export a complete JSON backup to your local hard drive or restore from one using the built-in Settings & Backup controls.

3. Permissions

RentReceipt requests zero special browser permissions. It does not request access to your browsing history, tabs, website data, or downloads. Everything runs locally in the extension's own interface.

4. Client-Side PDF Generation

All rent receipt PDFs are generated entirely on your device using a locally bundled, self-contained library (jsPDF). No documents, names, or financial numbers are ever sent across the internet.

5. Changes to This Policy

Because RentReceipt is an open-source, local-first extension that does not communicate with external services, our privacy commitments remain immutable. Any future software updates will maintain this local-first architecture.
